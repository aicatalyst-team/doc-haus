name: contract-risk-checklist
description: A checklist of the high-risk areas to inspect in a commercial contract. Use when reviewing a contract for risk.
---

When reviewing a commercial contract, inspect each of these areas and cite the
governing clause (or note its absence):

<checklist>
- **Liability** — caps, exclusions, carve-outs, mutual vs one-sided.
- **Indemnification** — scope, triggers, who indemnifies whom, defense control.
- **Termination** — for cause, for convenience, notice periods, cure rights, effect on fees.
- **Payment** — amounts, schedule, late fees, set-off, currency, taxes.
- **Intellectual property** — ownership, licenses, work product, residuals.
- **Confidentiality** — definition, duration, permitted disclosures, return/destruction.
- **Warranties** — express, disclaimers, duration.
- **Limitation of remedies** — exclusive remedies, consequential-damages waivers.
- **Assignment & change of control** — consent requirements.
- **Governing law & dispute resolution** — venue, arbitration, jury waiver.
- **Force majeure** — covered events, obligations during, termination thresholds.
- **Data protection** — processing roles, security obligations, breach notice.
</checklist>

For each area: state what the contract says, cite as `[<Document> § <section>]`,
and flag missing, one-sided, or ambiguous terms.
